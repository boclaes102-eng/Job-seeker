Send jobs from your browser

Drag this button to your bookmarks bar. Then click it on any job page, on LinkedIn, a company site or a job board, to add that job. If the job is already in your list, it gets the full posting text and is scored again.

Your CV

Every job is scored against these skills from your CV. Replace CV.pdf with a newer version and this list updates by itself.

How a score is built

35
Role. Is the job title one of the roles your CV points to: security, full stack, IoT?
30
Skills. How many of your skills the posting asks for. A related tool counts half, like Splunk when you know SIEM.
15
Distance. From the nearest of your home towns. Remote or hybrid adds a bonus.
10
Coverage. Of all skills the posting names, the share you have.
10
Freshness. How long ago the job was posted. Full points in the first 3 days, then less each week. After 3 weeks a job may already be filled.

Senior or lead titles, internships and jobs asking far more years than you have score lower. Junior and medior titles score higher.
